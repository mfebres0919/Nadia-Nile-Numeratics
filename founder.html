The Founder

Meet Nadia Higgins

Nadia Higgins is an engineer and researcher focused on intelligent mobility, autonomous systems, and human-centered engineering innovation. She is an Automotive Engineer for a leading global automaker, an MBA candidate at Louisiana State University Shreveport, and a graduate of Prairie View A&M University, where she earned a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering, magna cum laude.

She founded Nile Numeratics to bring the same precision and analytical rigor she applies to aerospace and automotive engineering into the classroom — increasing access to hands-on STEM experiences for the next generation of problem-solvers.

  • Automotive Engineer Leading global automaker
  • NASA & Space Force Undergraduate research assistant
  • Magna Cum Laude B.S. Mechanical Engineering

The same rigor that ships vehicles and spacecraft, brought to a student’s desk — precision in every variable.
